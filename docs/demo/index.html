THE PROBLEM

Commuting runs on habit, not information.

NO FEEDBACK

People choose how to travel without ever seeing the CO₂ their trip produced.

HARD TO JUDGE

Is walking or cycling realistic today? It depends on distance, weather and personal limits.

INSTITUTIONS NEED EVIDENCE

Universities and employers want engagement data — without exposing anyone’s routine.

69.2%of US workers drove alone in 2024
~28%of US greenhouse-gas emissions come from transportation (2022)
27.2 minmean one-way US commute

National figures quoted in our project proposal. They motivate the project; they do not measure local pilot demand or our own reduction.

MISSION & SYSTEM OF INTEREST
Help commuters understand their estimated impact and consider practical lower-carbon options — while giving institutions approved, privacy-safe engagement information.

SYSTEM OF INTEREST

C.0 EcoCommute — a lightweight responsive web application.

NEED 1.1.4 · GET ADVICE I CAN USE

“I need suggestions for a greener way to travel that fit the weather and my own limits, so I get advice I can actually follow.”

▶

REQ 2.1.4

The system shall provide, for each accepted trip with an eligible lower-emission option, one alternative travel mode with a stated reason based on trip distance, forecast weather and the Commuter’s feasibility settings.

REQ 2.1.5

The system shall exclude from its recommendations every travel mode that the Commuter has marked as not feasible.

HOW WE KNOW IT IS MET

2.1.5: 0 recommendations containing an excluded mode (rule test set). 2.1.4: 80%+ of surveyed commuters rate the advice feasible. Every requirement has one “shall”, a measurable target, a validation method, and a trace to a need and an action.
