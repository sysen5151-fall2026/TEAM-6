# Product risk register (EcoCommute, Team 6)

Chapter 12 product build. Each risk has an identifier of the Innoslate model (`TR.n`, model 11.5: `docs/model/EcoCommute_11.5_C11-C12_analysis_risk.xml`) and is cross-referenced to the flows, functions, assets and requirements it touches. Rows marked **AI** are specific to an AI-enabled product or to building with an assistant. The report that explains the method is `docs/ch11/EcoCommute_Team6_Ch11-Ch12_System_Analysis_and_Risk_Assessment.docx`.

**Likelihood and impact are the team's own engineering judgments**, made from the measurements named in each row. They were not generated by an assistant (the lab manual says a generated rating carries no information). Review them as a team and change a number in `docs/ch11/sim/model.py`, then rerun `python docs/ch11/build/make_risks_md.py`.

## Scales and calculation

**Likelihood (L)**: 1 Rare (not expected in the pilot) / 2 Unlikely (could happen once) / 3 Possible (expected a few times) / 4 Likely (expected most weeks) / 5 Almost certain (already observed or expected every day).

**Impact scales** (each 1 to 5):

| Rating | Technical (performance, correctness, trust) | Schedule (pilot results due 18 December 2026) | Cost (system baseline $750,000) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No requirement affected | No delay | Less than $500 |
| 2 | A requirement degraded for a minority of uses, with a labelled fallback | Under 1 day | $500 to $2,000 |
| 3 | A requirement missed in part of the pilot | 1 to 3 days | $2,000 to $10,000 |
| 4 | A shall requirement not met or a wrong statement shown to a commuter | 3 to 7 days | $10,000 to $50,000 |
| 5 | The core value proposition fails, or personal data is exposed | More than 7 days or the pilot window is missed | Over $50,000 |

**Overall impact** = 0.5 x technical + 0.25 x schedule + 0.25 x cost (the sponsor's measures are mostly about whether the product works, so technical counts double). **Risk level** = L x overall impact (1 to 25). **Bands**: High 12 or more (red), Medium 6 to under 12 (yellow), Low under 6 (green). On the matrix the impact axis uses the overall impact rounded to the nearest whole number.

## Top risk and what was built

The top risk is **TR.16 External service answers late and the 3-second display fails** (level 14.00). A **guardrail** was built, because the failure can be prevented: a call that has not answered by its deadline is dropped, so the plan is returned inside the 3-second display time, and the answer carries a **fallback** (a labelled estimate; the recommendation says weather rules were not applied). Both live in `src/c2_application_service/plan.py` and `src/c3_data_adapter/route_adapter.py`; the tests are in `tests/test_dispatch_guardrail.py`. The top AI-specific risk, TR.4, has its own guardrail and fallback in `src/c4_explanation_model/` (ADR 0011).

Monitoring indicators, review points and the model's role in tracking them are in the Chapter 11 and 12 report (Section 9).
